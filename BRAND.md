# Plentli brand guidelines

**Name:** Plentli

**Tagline:** Good food. More people.

**Personality:** Warm, capable, calm and direct. Food sharing should feel human; operational decisions should feel trustworthy.

Use `Plentli` with a capital P in every interface. Keep the Latin wordmark unmirrored in Arabic. The repo slug is different and should not appear as product copy. Name, domain and trademark availability have not yet been checked.

## Color

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forest | `#163D32` | Brand, header, primary actions |
| Green | `#21634B` | Selected states and accents |
| Ink | `#172D25` | Main text |
| Muted ink | `#56655D` | Secondary text |
| Ivory | `#F6F7F2` | Page background |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards |
| Sage | `#E7EFE5` | Quiet confirmation |
| Lime | `#D9EF91` | Sparing highlights, dark text only |
| Amber ink | `#8A4B0F` | Time, allergen and blocked labels |
| Amber surface | `#FFF1D6` | Time, allergen and blocked surfaces |
| Border | `#DCE5DE` | Dividers |

Color never acts alone: pair status color with clear text and an icon. Check final rendered contrast against WCAG AA.

## Type and imagery

Use **Manrope** for headings and the wordmark direction, **Inter** for interface text, and **Noto Sans Arabic** or another reviewed Arabic sans for RTL. Use system fallbacks. Body text is at least 16 px on mobile. Use tabular numerals for timers and ETAs.

The compact looped-leaf symbol in the boards is a brand concept, not a final vector asset. A designer should draw and approve the production logo; do not ship a raster crop from the mockups. Use one consistent outline icon family. Use actual donor food photos in the app. The food imagery in the boards is illustrative.

## Voice

Use short, specific actions: **Add a photo**, **Accept 30 meals**, **Decline offer**, **Confirm 30 meals received**. For uncertainty say **Waiting for replies**. For a refusal say **Dispatch paused**, then name the failed check and next step. Do not shame a recipient for declining. Avoid inflated impact claims and unsupported safety language.

Mark seeded content as **Demo data** and simulated messages as **Simulated messaging** wherever they could be mistaken for live integrations. Mark the mock deadline **Demo rule**. Environmental estimates remain **Not calculated** until supported by measured data and a cited factor.
